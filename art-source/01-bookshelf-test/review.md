### Fit with the existing layout

The `Bookshelf` slot is `clamp(130px,16%,210px)` × `clamp(280px,54%,440px)`,
with art locked to 150:320. A 1:1 shelf in that box either:
- fills the width and is half the height (210×210), leaving the left wall
  emptier than now, or
- fills the height and is 440 wide, colliding with the art wall and easel.

So this composition **can't drop into the scene** without a layout
change. It also doesn't match the tall shelf in `design-reference.png`.

### Verdict

**A good composition and content test, not yet the art-quality bar.** The
subject matter, palette, cat and plant are right and are worth keeping as
a reference for content. The rendering style (outlined, cel-shaded) and
the shelf proportions need another round. Recommended next step: v02 with
the revised prompt (`docs/art-generation-prompts.md` § T1 v02).
